### Sahifa 1

My favorite fonts
Hey there! In this list I wanted to share 11 free fonts that I really love, 
and honestly I can’t imagine being a designer without them. All of 
them are free, so you can use them in your client work and other 
commercial projects. Enjoy!
00
---
### Sahifa 2

Inter
Inter is a classic and minimalistic sans-serif typeface that can 
be used in pretty much any design. It’s very readable, and is 
even available as a variable font, so it’s really flexible. It looks 
close to a popular font family from Apple - San Francisco.
01
---
### Sahifa 3

Lato
Lato is another highly readable and clean font. It will work perfect for 
even very long chunks of text. It was designed so it works well both 
in small and large sizes, so like Inter it’s very versatile. The curves give 
it a warm feeling, yet it still looks serious enough.
02
---
### Sahifa 4

Manrope
Manrope reminds me of Inter, but it’s just a bit more playful and 
friendly. It might not work as well for long chunks of text as other 
typefaces from this list, but overall it’s a great font for UI Design. 
It’s also available as a variable font, so it’s very customisable.
03
---
### Sahifa 5

Rubik
Rubik is one of my favorite free fonts. It has a beautiful futuristic 
vibe, yet it’s still perfectly readable. It’s used by many well 
designed products, so it’s going to be a safe choice for your work 
as well. Oh, did I mention it’s a variable font too?
04
---
### Sahifa 6

Nunito
Nunito is probably the friendlest family from this list. It has slightly 
rounded edges, making it a perfect pick for any digital product that 
is supposed to feel “friendly”. It’s also available in it’s non-rounded 
variant called Nunito Sans. I prefer this one though!
05
---
### Sahifa 7

Work Sans
Initially I did not really like Work Sans that much, but after some 
time I think it looks fine. It’s even used by well-known 
companies, such as Figma. I’d suggest lowering the letter 
spacing to -2%, otherwise it looks a bit worse (in my opinion).
06
---
### Sahifa 8

Mulish
Looks familiar? Mulish is a typeface that’s very close in the way it 
looks to Proxima Nova - the typeface I used to write this eBook.
I absolutely love Mulish. It’s a readable, yet clean-looking typeface 
that should suit the majority of your UI Designs.
07
---
### Sahifa 9

Lora
Time for a classy serif font! Lora is one of my favorites. It’s easily 
readable and looks really good. It’s going to make a perfect pair to 
some sans-serifs from this list, such as Inter or Lato. It’s a variable 
font, so you don’t have to worry about weights!
08
---
### Sahifa 10

Gelasio
Gelasio looks really close to Lora, but for some reason it seems more 
readable for me, so it should be just fine for even longer bits of text. 
Sadly, it does not have that many weights and is not a variable font, 
but hopefully it will be enough for your projects.
09
---
### Sahifa 11

PT Serif
Even though it has only two weights - Regular and Bold, PT Serif is 
probably my favorite free serif typeface. It works fantastic for large 
headers as well as small paragraphs of text. To be honest, it’s hard to 
even tell a difference between PT Serif and Gelasio!
10
---
### Sahifa 12

Space Mono
Once every 100 projects you’ll have to use
a monospace typeface. This one is my favorite.
As you can see it’s not really that readable,
so it should not be used as a primary typeface.
11
